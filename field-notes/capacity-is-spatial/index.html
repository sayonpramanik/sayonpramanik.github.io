Capacity is spatial: thinking beyond a single crowd number

Beyond area-based capacity

Maximum occupancy is often calculated from usable floor area. For a Durga Puja pandal, that number tells only part of the story. Visitors arrive through a street network, queue at thresholds, circulate through a temporary structure and disperse into surrounding public space.

Capacity is therefore distributed. A narrow approach road, a sharp turn or a conflict between incoming and outgoing movement can become more important than the nominal area inside the pavilion.

A relational framework

The emerging dataset connects site geometry with access routes, entry and exit conditions, nearby intersections and observed movement patterns. Spatial measures can then be read alongside temporal variation and event management practices.

The aim is not to produce a universal “safe number.” It is to create a transparent framework that helps researchers and practitioners ask better, spatially specific questions.
