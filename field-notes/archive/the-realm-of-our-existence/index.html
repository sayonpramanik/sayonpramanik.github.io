The Realm of our Existence...

The senses which we are born with are just a membrane to keep our mind deceived from the reality and the self within.The metaphysics resolving the ontology of the fundamentalism of our existence. The co-ordinates of the dimensions for the referral position are just like the essence being spread in the vacuum which has a specific pattern of spreading out and the absolution of epistemology. The philosophy of natural theology is complex to its very core.

It has always been hovering in the mind regarding the realm as an extent of Universe. Before we are aware of who & what we are; the question is where we are! The defined space has no limitation no boundaries. The science is what we see but beyond that can't be stated as imagination or virtual. The first principle of Para consistent logic makes us believe what we are rather than what we see. But the sub conscious mind reflects the quest of that vast space unanswered by modern science. This is something as being stated by most of the scientists as legitimation of our thoughts. Trying to analyse it through what we have heard since years is 'The absolute truth'. As being expected through our mind its the biological process of chemical reactions. We define the inconsistency tolerance as the matter of time where we rise above our consciousness to define what is finite regardless of its infinity. So to define the existence in this endless string is just the abstraction of dilemma between the self & the consciousness. It can only be understood as the specific position of every individual in this system where relating one through the other is just like the node from the start of all.
