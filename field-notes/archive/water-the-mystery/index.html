WATER : The Mystery...

Archive note

Originally published in 2010. This text is preserved as an early personal reflection and does not represent current scholarly claims or research findings.

View the original Blogger post ↗

AS THE MYSTICAL EXPERIENCES, THE RECURRENT GLIOMAS, FELT THAT ' WATER IS POISON FOR US ' ,IT IS THE THING WHICH SEPARATES FROM BEING GOD, IT KEEPS US UP IN ILLUSION OF THIS WORLDLY POSSESSIONS...

Upon the response of this thought i will give my further justifications...

After the long wait i thought i must explain the philosophy ....

WATER AS WE SAY IS THE LIFE GIVING ELEMENT....
WE ARE TRYING TO SEARCH FOR WATER ON OTHER PLANETS SO AS TO SEE LIFE ON THEM BUT IF WE TRY TO GO BACK TO HISTORY WATER HAS BEEN A REASON FOR WAR,
NOT ONLY THAT LETS GO BACK FEW MORE CENTURIES, WHERE ACCORDING TO MYTHOLOGY WATER WAS CHURNED TO PRODUCE 'AMRIT' OR A THING WHICH CAN MAKE US IMMORTAL....
HOPE YOU ALL MUST KNOW WHEN 'VISHNU' GOD TRICKED THE 'ASURAS' TO GIVE THEM SOMETHING ELSE THAN AMRIT.LET US MAKE THIS SIMPLE LIKE AS WELL FEEL WE NEED WATER TO SURVIVE ...IF IN COMING GENERATIONS IF THERE IS NO WATER THEN A SPECIES WILL EVOLVE WHICH CAN SURVIVE WITHOUT WATER, & DEFINITELY IF WE SEE TODAY'S 'YOGIS' OR 'SADHUS' WE WILL DEFINITELY FEEL THE SAME THING...
THIS IS A ATTRACTION FOR US WHICH BOUND US TO THIS WORLD, THE WORLD OF DEAD...
COMING TO SCIENCE H2 & O2 AMONGST THEM ONE HELPS IN BURNING & THE OTHER BURNS ITSELF BUT SEE THE UNIQUE COMBINATION WHERE THEY ARE SO CALLED PROVIDING LIFE...
IS THAT POSSIBLE...
MAY BE THERE IS A WORLD WHERE ALL OUR LAWS OF PHYSICS FAIL...
AGAIN TO BE PRECISE :-
THERE ARE A LOT OF DISEASE WHICH ARE PRODUCED FROM WATER...
IF THAT IS TO BE THE BEST COMPOUND EVER CREATED IN THIS UNIVERSE THEN IT IS AN OPEN QUESTION TO YOU ALL :-
'IS WATER ONLY THE UNIVERSAL SOLVENT ? '

IT IS TOUGH TO MAKE US BELIEVE OR SAY WHAT IS NOT POSSIBLE BUT TO SOME EXTENT IF THE RESEARCHERS IMPLY THIS FACT OF LIFE THEN THEY WOULD DEFINITELY CONCLUDE THAT IT IS SOMETHING WHICH MAKE US MORTAL...

try to analyse & give Ur comments as this may be a turning point of the history of human race....
