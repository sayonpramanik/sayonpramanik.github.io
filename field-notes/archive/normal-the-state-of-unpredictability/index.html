Normal: The state of Unpredictability

Archive note

Originally published in 2010. This text is preserved as an early personal reflection and does not represent current scholarly claims or research findings.

View the original Blogger post ↗

The hydrocarbon having unbalanced chain chemically reacting in our body to form the mind.There have been a lot of experiments on our mental health & status.But its really tough to analyse what, when & how!Our mind the most mysterious thing ever, could never resolve its own manufacturing & working process. There have been several theories trying to define the 'para', 'ab' and 'transit' state, as the most common ones.

The being of one self, is the state of perfection. The art which all human has been inhibited in the psyche. The Chaos & culmination of thoughts generating the hyper complex scenarios giving rise to 'deja vu' or 'jamais vu', which no one can neglect for the first case & never care for the second case.Its really astonishing to know that such are different kinds of epilepsy. The crypt amnesia is not the things which we might know but they invoke in our own self, explaining such is weird like a place's name or some word in different language which you never learnt. The mythology of Hindus & the Greek Gods resembles kinda same feature, or even in case of The Mayan Deities. But these civilisations were far from each other & even at different times.So it was well understood that like this Universe which has endlessness has a realm like our mind where it repeats, reciprocates & confides in the sublimated strata of undefined dreaming space of 'Bramha', the scriptures of Vedas has some astonishing facts with resemblance to the above stated things.So in short the existence is just the dream.A statement which many a times concluded at many places but where does these 'para' activities come from? People experience them at different places, different time! Something which make them super human as their sensor's go more responsive the environment around, controlling them lead or guide them to a well defined space of newness, may be a place which is next to what we call 'param satya'. The 'Tantrics' have been practising this act of control for several thousand years, but no one knows whether they succeed or its just screen which we look at.Such activities are sometimes called as energised waveforms but are controversial description conflicting the sixth sense.

Though its not a mere thing to talk on, but an issue which can lead us to learn & process the things to give it a better shape for the future to come up.The technologies say 'Ghosts' can be heard through emf, others say light forms, but no one care to see the root of cause the 'NORMALITY' of human mind which has the immense potential to create Universe of its own showing the illusion to the forth comers. The supreme is the positioned psychology of all these facts, in short 'moksha'. The state of 'para' & 'ab', beyond the confined limits of normal. The String in the theory was the link of self & psychology.

THIS IS SOMETHING WHICH HAS BEEN HOVERING MY MIND;DOODLING MY IMAGINATIONS;EXTENDING IT TO SEE THE POSSIBILITIES TO UNDERSTAND IT FOR SPACE TIME, THE BASIC THROUGH WHICH WE TRY TO KEEP OURSELVES ASSURED OF THE CO-ORDINATES OF OUR EXISTENCE, THE REAL BEING OR NORMALITY...

DO TRY TO MERGE THE THINGS,UNDERSTAND THE UNSTATED SIDE & THINK UPON IT...
