Biography

Sayon Pramanik is a Doctoral Researcher at The Bartlett School of Architecture, University College London. His work sits at the intersection of architecture, urban research, spatial analysis and computational methods.

His research examines how spatial configuration influences pedestrian movement, accessibility, collective behaviour and spatial culture in post-colonial cities. He is particularly interested in processional routes, inherited urban structures, public space and the relationship between computational evidence and lived urban experience.

Sayon welcomes collaborations involving space syntax, behavioural mapping, GIS, GeoAI, urban morphology, cultural routes and computational urban research.
