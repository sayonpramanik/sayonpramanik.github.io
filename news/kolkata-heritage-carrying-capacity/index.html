Carrying capacity in Kolkata’s heritage core

My chapter with Sampurna Biswas, “Evaluating Carrying Capacity of Heritage Core of Post-Colonial City: Case of Kolkata, India”, was published online on 2 January 2026 in Urban Sustainable Solutions, pages 105–126.

The study examines how street configuration and mobility demands interact with heritage preservation. The publisher’s abstract describes Space Syntax analysis, figure-ground mapping and pedestrian catchments linked to bus-stop accessibility. It reports constraints associated with poor connectivity and congestion, and considers route diversions through scenario modelling.

A question for heritage planning

The wider question is how changes to circulation can support everyday access while retaining the spatial relationships that give a historic district its character. This is a reflection prompted by the publication, rather than a claim that proposed interventions have been implemented.

This note revisits the January publication; it is not an announcement of a newly published chapter.
