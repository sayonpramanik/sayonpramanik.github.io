2020 Best Paper Award for Linga Raj Temple study

This note records an award from 2020. NIT Raipur’s April 2020 Mirror newsletter reports that I received a Best Paper Award at the International Conference on History and Theory of Architecture in Zurich on 13 January 2020.

The award was for “Accessibility and Visibility through Space Syntax Analysis of the Linga Raj Temple in Odisha, India.” The paper examines temple visibility and accessibility through point isovist studies and Visibility Graph Analysis.

The institute’s newsletter records the award, and the publication record describes the study.
