Placemaking article included in a geography syllabus

The geography syllabus for semesters VII and VIII hosted by Shaheed Bhagat Singh College, University of Delhi, lists “Understanding Local Culture as an Attribute for Placemaking in India” (2024), by Priya Sangani and Sayon Pramanik.

The reference appears in Geography of Behaviour and Emotions (DSE 15), under the tutorial exercise for Unit 4: Placemaking practices, on PDF page 18. This documents inclusion in a reading list; it does not establish how the article has been taught or constitute an institutional endorsement.

The source was checked on 24 September 2026. This update records that verification date, rather than claiming the syllabus was released today.
